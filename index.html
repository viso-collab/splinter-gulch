What’s inside

Every piggy you smash has a small chance to drop a chest. Iron, Banker and Golden Piggies drop chests more often. Getting an ability you already own levels it up (max 5). The day clock pauses while you open chests.

Abilities

You can only equip one ability at a time.

Piggies

New piggies show up as you clear days. Later piggies are tougher but pay a lot more, and every piggy has its own tricks. The yard mixes in your four newest piggies.
